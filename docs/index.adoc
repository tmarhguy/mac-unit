== SRAM-based 4-bit MAC

A 6T bitcell, a 16×4 SRAM, and a 4-bit MAC in Cadence GPDK 18 nm at 0.8 V. Seventeen cycles from START to DONE.

[.lead-metrics]
0.39 µm² cell · 0.69 ns SRAM · 2.5 ns MAC. Part 1 is extracted; Parts 2 and 3 are schematic-level.

image::bitcell-schematic.png[Symmetric six transistor SRAM schematic in Virtuoso,align=center,width=320]

The unit computes stem:[\mathrm{ACC}=\sum_{i=0}^{15} W_i X_i] from two copies of the Part 2 macro, a 4×4 array multiplier, a 12-bit ripple accumulator, and a 4-bit counter plus FSM. Every register is a transmission-gate master-slave flip-flop. ESE 5700 Project 1, University of Pennsylvania.

Start with the <<spec,specification>>, then the <<sizing,bitcell>>, the <<macro,SRAM macro>>, and the <<mac-system,MAC>>. Estimates are labeled. Full write-ups:
