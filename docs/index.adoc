== A 16-element MAC around two SRAM macros

ESE 5700 Project 1 (Digital Integrated Circuits and VLSI Fundamentals, University of Pennsylvania) is a 6T SRAM bitcell, a 16×4 synchronous SRAM macro, and an SRAM-based 4-bit multiply-accumulate unit in *Cadence GPDK 18 nm* (`cds_ff_mpt`) at nominal stem:[V_{DD}=0.8\,\mathrm V]. The unit computes stem:[\mathrm{ACC}=\sum_{i=0}^{15} W_i X_i] from two copies of the Part 2 macro, a 4×4 array multiplier, a 12-bit ripple accumulator, and a 4-bit counter plus FSM. All registers are transmission-gate master-slave flip-flops. A pipelined read / MAC takes 17 cycles from START to DONE.

Selected operating points (schematic-level on the MAC; Part 1 cell is extracted):

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Part | Result
| **1** bitcell | 0.72 µm × 0.54 µm = 0.39 µm², D=28, DRC 0, LVS clean; extracted read SNM 140 mV at 0.8 V
| **2** SRAM macro | stem:[T_{CLK}=0.69] ns, stem:[t_{\mathrm{access}}=488] ps, area 54.6 µm² (est.), FOM stem:[\approx 4.7\times10^{3}]
| **3** MAC | stem:[T_{CLK}=2.5] ns (400 MHz), stem:[t_{dot}=42.5] ns, Test A stem:[E_{dot}=8.870] pJ, FOM~MAC~ stem:[=1.1\times10^{5}] µm²·pJ·ns
|===
