== A six-transistor memory cell

This is the circuit that stores one bit inside a memory chip: six transistors, two of them holding the bit, the others letting a read or a write through. It was drawn for ESE 5700, a course in analog integrated circuits, in Cadence's 18 nm process. The cell is the first piece of a later 16 by 4 memory array.

The cell was drawn in *Cadence GPDK 18 nm* (`cds_ff_mpt`) at nominal stem:[V_{DD}=0.8\,\mathrm V]. The selected cell is β=2, γ=1 (PD 4 fins, PU 2 fins, PG 2 fins, L=18 nm). It measures 0.72 µm × 0.54 µm, or 0.39 µm² = 3900 stem:[F^2], with density factor D = 28. Pegasus DRC is 0 violations and LVS is clean, with comparison match. Extracted loads are stem:[C_{BL}=180] aF and stem:[C_{WL}=90] aF. At 0.8 V the extracted hold SNM is 280 mV, read SNM is 140 mV, the disturbed zero node is 95 mV, write margin is 0.42 V, and cell current is 25 µA.

|===

The selected cell has β=2 and pull-up ratio γ=1. The 29.7 mV improvement over β=1 is the schematic result at 0.8 V. Extraction lowers read SNM to 140 mV. At 0.5 V the cell still reads (70 mV) and the write is the weak result (stem:[\mathrm{WM}_{BL}=0.18] V).
