[preface]
== MAC at a glance

SRAM-based 4-bit multiply-accumulate (MAC) accelerator for
ESE 5700 — two 16 × 4 SRAM macros, a 4×4 multiplier, a 12-bit
accumulator, and a sequencer computing a 16-element dot product
in the course 45 nm Cadence Virtuoso / Spectre flow.

.6T SRAM bitcell schematic in Virtuoso — `proj1_lib` 6t-sram
image::bitcell-schematic.png[6T SRAM bitcell schematic canvas,align=center,width=432]

++++
<div style="text-align:center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<a href="https://github.com/tmarhguy/mac-unit" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="display:inline-block;background:#24292f;color:#ffffff;font-size:20px;font-weight:700;padding:14px 36px;border-radius:10px;text-decoration:none;margin:6px;">GitHub Repository</a>
</div>
++++

The unit computes:

[stem]
++++
\mathrm{ACC} = \sum_{i=0}^{15} W[i] \cdot X[i]
++++

over 4-bit unsigned weights and activations. Part 1 sizes and
characterizes the 6T bitcell; Part 2 tiles it into a 16 × 4 macro;
Part 3 wires two macros through the multiplier, accumulator, and
sequencer. Dive in with <<spec,Specification>> for the full breakdown,
or <<bitcell,Bitcell>> for the Part 1 design as it stands.
