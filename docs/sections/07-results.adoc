== Read-SNM experiment and evidence

=== Actual testbenches

For each β, the read bench holds WL, BL and BLB at VDD=0.8 V. One DC bench sweeps Q and records QB; the other sweeps QB and records Q with feedback broken. The two original Virtuoso testbench captures below document the biasing and signals used.

.Q-sweep read-SNM bench
image::read_snm_q_tb_sch.png[Original Virtuoso Q sweep testbench with read biases,align=center,width=700]

.QB-sweep read-SNM bench
image::read_snm_qb_tb_sch.png[Original Virtuoso QB sweep testbench with read biases,align=center,width=700]

The four-column ViVA `raw.csv` for each β is split into `q.csv` and `qb.csv`; one curve is reflected about stem:[y=x]. `scripts/check_snm_sweep.py` finds the three intersections and fits the largest voltage square in each butterfly lobe. The smaller lobe side is stem:[SNM_{read}]. Its final 16,001-point computation differs from a 4,001-point check by at most 0.05 mV.

[cols="1,1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| β | PD fins | SNM left/right | Read SNM | Low crossing
| 1.0 | 2 | 126.6 / 126.6 mV | 126.6 mV | 134.3 mV
| 1.5 | 3 | 145.8 / 145.8 mV | 145.8 mV | 96.6 mV
| *2.0* | *4* | *156.3 / 156.3 mV* | *156.3 mV* | *77.1 mV*
|===

At the handout's two-significant-figure precision, read SNM is 0.13, 0.15 and 0.16 V; the disturbed zero-node crossings are 0.13, 0.097 and 0.077 V. The table retains extra digits to trace the calculations.

The low-voltage crossing is the reported DC zero-node read-disturb operating point. The β=2 schematic improves read SNM by 29.7 mV (23%) over β=1 at 0.8 V, while lowering that crossing by 57.2 mV.

=== Data trail

`results/part1/read-snm/beta*/raw.csv` is the verbatim ViVA export. The normalized `q.csv` and `qb.csv`, computed `snm.json` and clean butterfly PNG/SVG are reproducible derivatives. Run:

[source,bash]
----
python3 scripts/check_snm_sweep.py --all
----

The clean plots are secondary reading aids; the screenshots above are the original Cadence view. This sweep is schematic. The extracted read SNM that supersedes 156.3 mV is 140 mV, in <<signoff,post-layout stability>>.
