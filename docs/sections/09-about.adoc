== Sources, reproducibility and authors

This site is the HTML companion to the three Gradescope PDFs:

* link:report.pdf[Part 1] — 6T cell, layout, extracted SNM
* link:report-part2.pdf[Part 2] — 16×4 SRAM macro
* link:report-part3.pdf[Part 3] — MAC at stem:[T_{CLK}=2.5] ns

The assignment source is the link:ese5700-project1.pdf[nine-page handout]. Part 1 due 10/13/2026, Part 2 10/20/2026, Part 3 10/27/2026. Cadence libraries stay on the cluster (`proj1_lib`); this repository keeps reports, figures, and scripts.

Part 1 SNM CSVs are under `results/part1/read-snm/beta1/`, `beta1p5/` and `beta2/` (`raw.csv`, transfer curves, `snm.json`). Cyan overlay coordinates come from the two `lobes` entries in each `snm.json`; the fitted margin is the smaller side. The script uses 16,001 voltage samples and checks against 4,001 samples. Images in `media/part1/` retain the handwritten work and original ViVA views.

Part 2 cropped captures: `report/part2/img/small/`. Part 3 ViVA captures: `report/part3/img/`. This site stages a curated subset, not every dump.

Tyrone Marhguy and Victor Wanjohi are the report authors. Both are responsible for the whole design and report. Task split is in <<signoff,team contributions>>.
