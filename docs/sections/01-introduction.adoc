== Assignment and evidence

The link:ese5700-project1.pdf[current handout] sets the project in GPDK 18 nm at nominal 0.8 V and requires one team PDF per part. Part 1 asks for a symmetric cell, hand sizing, a three-value schematic β sweep, a DRC/LVS-clean tileable layout, parasitic extraction, post-layout stability, supply dependence and a functional write/read. Parts 2 and 3 add the 16×4 macro and the MAC. Each report names both members and their contributions.

=== What is recorded

* Virtuoso 6T schematic with PD/PG/PU fin counts; handwritten read/write analysis; β=1 / 1.5 / 2 SNM CSVs and original ViVA butterflies.
* Layout area, DRC 0, LVS match, extracted stem:[C_{BL}=180] aF / stem:[C_{WL}=90] aF, post-layout SNM and write margin (<<signoff>>).
* Part 2 macro benches: write driver nfin=8, decoder, `tb_sram_macro` access and power (<<macro>>).
* Part 3 `tb_top` Tests A/B/C, DFF characterization, multiplier 256/256, controller 60/60, energy and FOM (<<mac-system>>).

=== Evidence level

Part 1 numbers on this site that come from Quantus are extracted. Part 2 and Part 3 timing, energy and area (except the tiled cell) are schematic-level or stem:[D\sum W_i L_i] estimates and are labeled as such. A hand estimate never stands in for a measured Spectre integral.
