== Assignment and evidence

The link:ese5700-project1.pdf[current handout] sets Part 1 in GPDK 18 nm at nominal 0.8 V and requires one team report. It asks for a symmetric cell, hand sizing analysis, a three-value schematic β sweep, a DRC/LVS-clean tileable layout, parasitic extraction, post-layout stability measurements, supply dependence and a functional write/read test. The report must also identify each member's contribution.

=== What is recorded

* Virtuoso cell schematic with PD/PG/PU fin counts and equal halves.
* Handwritten read-path and current-balance work; typed read and write derivations.
* Two read-SNM DC testbench schematics, raw four-column Cadence exports, derived curves and machine-readable SNM calculations for β=1, 1.5 and 2.
* Original ViVA butterfly screenshots. Cyan boxes in this site show the maximum-square locations computed from the CSVs.
* A disconnected placement draft, clearly labeled as an unfinished layout.

=== What is still required

The repo has no completed cell boundary or DRC/LVS logs, no extracted cell or per-cell BL/WL capacitance, and no post-layout hold/read SNM, write margin, cell current, 0.7/0.5 V supply sweep, or functional waveforms. The handout's Part 2 and Part 3 specifications are project context; they are not completed results here.

All numbers on this site carry their actual evidence level. A hand estimate and a schematic sweep never stand in for a post-layout measurement.
