== Layout, extraction and post-layout results

The numbers below are the same ones in the link:report.pdf[PDF]. DRC and LVS are pass/fail: a clean cell is 0 violations and a match, not a partial marker count.

=== Floorplan

The drawn cell keeps the schematic sizes: L=18 nm, pull-ups at 2 fins, pull-downs at 4 fins, pass-gates at 2 fins.

* Two pull-ups in the top row, each contacted to a horizontal VDD rail.
* Two pull-downs in the bottom row, tied by a horizontal VSS rail.
* stem:[Q] and stem:[\overline Q] running horizontally between the cross-coupled devices.
* WL along the bottom edge and up both sides of the cell.

Mirrored neighbours can share the VDD rail, the VSS rail, and the bitline contacts. The PR boundary is 0.72 µm by 0.54 µm.

=== Density factor

The handout estimates un-laid-out blocks as stem:[A_{\mathrm{est}}=D\sum_i W_i L_i], with stem:[D] measured on this cell. One fin counts as the PDK fin pitch, stem:[W_{\mathrm{fin}}=48\,\mathrm{nm}], and stem:[L=18\,\mathrm{nm}] on every device. The cell has 16 fins, so

[stem]
++++
\sum_{\mathrm{cell}} W_i L_i = 16 \times 0.048 \times 0.018\;\mu\mathrm m^2 = 0.0138\;\mu\mathrm m^2,\qquad D = 0.39 / 0.0138 \approx 28
++++

The same fin-pitch convention has to be used for every stem:[\sum W_i L_i] in Parts 2 and 3.

=== Extraction

Parasitics come from Cadence Quantus 23.1.2 on `6t_sram_final_design` (typical, 25 °C, `rc_decoupled`, ground net VSS). The run terminated normally and wrote `av_extracted`: 4 `n1svt` + 2 `p1svt`, 132 R, 97 C. Node values are the Quantus All-Parasitics sums (decoupled + coupled + self C). They are net parasitics and do not necessarily include transistor-intrinsic C.

=== Post-layout stability at 0.8 V

These benches use the extracted view. The margin is the same largest-square reduction as the schematic sweep. If the butterfly is asymmetric, SNM is the smaller side.

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Item | Bench | Result
| Hold SNM | WL=0; feedback broken; both VTCs overlaid | 280 mV
| Read SNM | WL=stem:[V_{DD}], BL=BLB=stem:[V_{DD}] | 140 mV
| Read "0" node | DC voltage of the disturbed zero node; schematic crossing was 77.1 mV | 95 mV
| Write margin | Store Q=1; WL=stem:[V_{DD}]; BLB held at stem:[V_{DD}]; sweep BL from stem:[V_{DD}] to 0 | 0.42 V
| Cell current | Both bitlines at stem:[V_{DD}]; current from the zero-side bitline | 25 µA
|===

Extraction lowers read SNM from the schematic 156.3 mV to 140 mV and raises the disturbed zero node from 77.1 mV to 95 mV. Hold SNM, 280 mV, stays well above the read margin. The hold butterfly, extracted read butterfly, and write-margin sweep captures are not in the repository yet.

.Read SNM and write margin versus supply, extracted view
[cols="1,1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| stem:[V_{DD}] (V) | SNM~read~ (mV) | stem:[\mathrm{WM}_{BL}] (V) | Comment
| 0.8 | 140 | 0.42 | nominal; supersedes the 156.3 mV schematic result
| 0.7 | 110 | 0.32 |

Read SNM falls from 140 mV to 110 mV to 70 mV as the supply drops. stem:[\mathrm{WM}_{BL}] falls from 0.42 V to 0.32 V to 0.18 V. At 0.5 V the cell still reads. The write is the weak result: 0.18 V is still a trip point, and it is the smallest margin in the table.

=== Functional check

The transient bench drives WL and the precharge devices with ideal pulses and loads each bitline with the extracted cell plus 15 copies of 497.8 aF, standing in for a 16-row column. The sequence is write 0, read, write 1, read. The capture (`media/part1/functional_check.png`) is not in the repository yet.

=== What limits the cell

Read stability is set by the pass-gate/pull-down divider. The schematic sweep moved the disturbed zero node from 134 mV (β=1) to 77 mV (β=2). Extraction gives 140 mV of read SNM and a 95 mV zero node at 0.8 V, against a hold SNM of 280 mV, so the read is what the Part 2 noise budget has to clear. Quantus net parasitics are stem:[C_{BL}=497.8] aF, stem:[C_{BLB}=576.5] aF and stem:[C_{WL}=938.9] aF. Writability is the γ=1 limit: stem:[\mathrm{WM}_{BL}] is 0.42 V at 0.8 V and 0.18 V at 0.5 V. The cell still reads at 0.5 V (70 mV). The write is the weak one, so a Part 2 remedy, if that margin is not enough for the column, is a weaker pull-up or a write assist rather than a new cell. Part 2 keeps this cell, mirrors it to share the BL contacts and the VDD/VSS rails, and treats 7.97 fF / 3.76 fF as the cell-only column/row estimates until `6t_sram_64b` is extracted.
