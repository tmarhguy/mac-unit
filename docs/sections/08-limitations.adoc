== Layout, extraction and post-layout results

The numbers below are the same ones in the link:report.pdf[PDF]. DRC and LVS are pass/fail: a clean cell is 0 violations and a match, not a partial marker count.

=== Floorplan

The drawn cell keeps the schematic sizes: L=18 nm, pull-ups at 2 fins, pull-downs at 4 fins, pass-gates at 2 fins.

* Two pull-ups in the top row, each contacted to a horizontal VDD rail.
* Two pull-downs in the bottom row, tied by a horizontal VSS rail.
* stem:[Q] and stem:[\overline Q] running horizontally between the cross-coupled devices.
* WL along the bottom edge and up both sides of the cell.

Mirrored neighbours can share the VDD rail, the VSS rail, and the bitline contacts. The PR boundary is 0.72 µm by 0.54 µm.

=== Density factor

The handout estimates un-laid-out blocks as stem:[A_{\mathrm{est}}=D\sum_i W_i L_i], with stem:[D] measured on this cell. One fin counts as the PDK fin pitch, stem:[W_{\mathrm{fin}}=48\,\mathrm{nm}], and stem:[L=18\,\mathrm{nm}] on every device. The cell has 16 fins, so

[stem]
++++
\sum_{\mathrm{cell}} W_i L_i = 16 \times 0.048 \times 0.018\;\mu\mathrm m^2 = 0.0138\;\mu\mathrm m^2,\qquad D = 0.39 / 0.0138 \approx 28
++++

The same fin-pitch convention has to be used for every stem:[\sum W_i L_i] in Parts 2 and 3.

=== Extraction

Parasitics come from the layout with the tutorial Quantus flow (C+CC).

.Extracted node capacitance of one cell (WL = 0, bitlines held at stem:[V_{DD}])
[cols="2,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Node | Capacitance | Meaning for Part 2
| BL (one bitline, one cell) | 180 aF | stem:[C_{BL} \approx 16\times 180] aF, plus wire, per column
| WL (one cell) | 90 aF | stem:[C_{WL} \approx 4\times 90] aF, plus wire, per row
|===

The bitline term is the pass-gate drain junction plus the shared bitline contact. The wordline term is two pass-gate gates plus the WL wire across the cell. Fifteen copies of 180 aF are the stand-in column load in the functional check.

=== Post-layout stability at 0.8 V

These benches use the extracted view. The margin is the same largest-square reduction as the schematic sweep. If the butterfly is asymmetric, SNM is the smaller side.

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Item | Bench | Result
| Hold SNM | WL=0; feedback broken; both VTCs overlaid | 280 mV
| Read SNM | WL=stem:[V_{DD}], BL=BLB=stem:[V_{DD}] | 140 mV
| Read "0" node | DC voltage of the disturbed zero node; schematic crossing was 77.1 mV | 95 mV
| Write margin | Store Q=1; WL=stem:[V_{DD}]; BLB held at stem:[V_{DD}]; sweep BL from stem:[V_{DD}] to 0 | 0.42 V
| Cell current | Both bitlines at stem:[V_{DD}]; current from the zero-side bitline | 25 µA
|===

Extraction lowers read SNM from the schematic 156.3 mV to 140 mV and raises the disturbed zero node from 77.1 mV to 95 mV. Hold SNM, 280 mV, stays well above the read margin.

=== Functional check

The transient bench drives WL and the precharge devices with ideal pulses and loads each bitline with the extracted cell plus 15 copies of 180 aF, standing in for a 16-row column. The sequence is write 0, read, write 1, read. The capture (`media/part1/functional_check.png`) is not in the repository yet.

=== What limits the cell

Read stability is set by the pass-gate/pull-down divider. The schematic sweep moved the disturbed zero node from 134 mV (β=1) to 77 mV (β=2). Extraction gives 140 mV of read SNM and a 95 mV zero node at 0.8 V, against a hold SNM of 280 mV, so the read is what the Part 2 noise budget has to clear. The four-fin pull-down sets the 0.54 µm cell height and most of stem:[C_{WL}=90] aF. The two-fin pass-gate sets stem:[C_{BL}=180] aF and the 25 µA read current. Writability is the γ=1 limit: stem:[\mathrm{WM}_{BL}] is 0.42 V at 0.8 V and 0.18 V at 0.5 V. The cell still reads at 0.5 V (70 mV). The write is the weak one, so a Part 2 remedy, if that margin is not enough for the column, is a weaker pull-up or a write assist rather than a new cell. Part 2 keeps this cell, mirrors it to share the BL contacts and the VDD/VSS rails, and sizes the wordline driver and precharge from 16 × 180 aF and 4 × 90 aF plus wire.
