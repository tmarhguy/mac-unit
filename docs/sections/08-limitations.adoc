== Layout, extraction and post-layout results

Entries shown as — are not yet measured. The same blanks appear in the link:report.pdf[PDF].

=== Floorplan and tiling

The cell is laid out in `proj1_lib/6t-sram_final_layout/layout` (`cds_ff_mpt`, Virtuoso IC23.1 Layout XL) against `proj1_lib/6t-sram/schematic`. The six PDK FinFET P-cells stay at the schematic settings: L=18 nm, one finger, pull-ups PM0/PM1 at 2 fins, pull-downs NM0/NM1 at 4 fins, pass-gates NM3/NM2 at 2 fins.

* The two pull-ups share one n-well strip under the top VDD rail. The pull-downs and pass-gates sit in the p-substrate below it, so a vertically mirrored neighbour shares the rail and the well edge.
* The wordline runs horizontally across the cell, and both pass-gate gates tap it, so abutted cells form one continuous WL.
* BL and BLB run vertically on the metal above WL, at the left and right edges over the pass-gate drain contacts, so a mirrored neighbour shares each bitline contact.
* VSS is on the bottom edge and VDD on the top edge. stem:[Q] and stem:[\overline Q] stay internal.

Placement was generated from the schematic (Layout XL _Generate All From Source_, then _Place As In Schematic_) and snapped to the PDK fin grid by hand. Signal nets were routed with the Virtuoso Space-based Router on local interconnect and M1/M2; routes that broke LiAct or M1 end-of-line rules were redrawn by hand. Verification uses the course Pegasus 23.2 DRC and LVS decks under `proj1_lib/lvs`, not the preliminary _Verify → Process Rules_ check.

The final layout capture (`media/part1/6t_sram_layout_final.png`) and the Pegasus summary capture (`media/part1/pegasus_drc_lvs_summary.png`) are not in the repository yet.

=== Density factor

The handout estimates un-laid-out blocks as stem:[A_{\mathrm{est}}=D\sum_i W_i L_i], with stem:[D] measured on this cell. One fin counts as the PDK fin pitch, stem:[W_{\mathrm{fin}}=48\,\mathrm{nm}], and stem:[L=18\,\mathrm{nm}] on every device. The cell has 16 fins, so

[stem]
++++
\sum_{\mathrm{cell}} W_i L_i = 16 \times 0.048 \times 0.018\;\mu\mathrm m^2 = 0.0138\;\mu\mathrm m^2
++++

and stem:[D = A_{\mathrm{cell}} / 0.0138\,\mu\mathrm m^2]. The same fin-pitch convention has to be used for every stem:[\sum W_i L_i] in Parts 2 and 3.

=== Extraction

Parasitics come from the layout with the tutorial Quantus flow (C+CC, extracted view `6t-sram_final_layout/av_extracted`). The stability and functional checks use that view.

=== Functional check

The transient bench drives WL and the precharge devices with ideal pulses and loads each bitline with the extracted cell plus 15 copies of the per-cell bitline capacitance, standing in for a 16-row column. The sequence is write 0, read, write 1, read. The capture (`media/part1/functional_check.png`) is not in the repository yet. A passing run leaves stem:[Q] and stem:[\overline Q] undisturbed on both reads, and both writes finish inside the WL pulse.

=== What limits the cell

Read stability is set by the pass-gate/pull-down divider. The schematic sweep moved the disturbed zero node from 134 mV (β=1) to 77 mV (β=2) and raised read SNM by 30 mV. The extracted read SNM in the table above is the value that has to clear the Part 2 noise budget. The cost of β=2 is the four-fin pull-down, which sets the cell height and most of stem:[C_{WL}], and the two-fin pass-gate, which sets stem:[C_{BL}] and the read current the Part 2 sense path integrates. Writability depends on γ=1 and is established by the write-margin sweep. If stem:[\mathrm{WM}_{BL}] at 0.5 V is marginal, the Part 2 remedy is a weaker pull-up or a write assist in the column circuits, so this cell characterization stays valid. Part 2 keeps this cell, mirrors it to share BL contacts and the VDD/VSS rails, and sizes the wordline driver and precharge from stem:[16\times C_{BL}] and stem:[4\times C_{WL}] plus wire.
