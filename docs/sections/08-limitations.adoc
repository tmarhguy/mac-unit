== Known Limitations

Honest limits. Anything here is a candidate for the next part —
not a silent caveat.

* Bitcell Cadence screenshots, TB schematics, and clean butterfly plots live in `media/part1/`; raw CSVs and `snm.json` in `results/part1/read-snm/beta*/`.
* Schematic read-SNM is measured (β1 126.6mV, β1.5 145.8mV, β2 156.3mV selected); hold/write/cell-current, extraction, and all post-layout tables are `TBD` until Spectre benches run on the extracted netlist.
* Macro and MAC chapters are structural stubs for Parts 2–3.
* Legacy 45nm `results/part1/gpdk45/`, `corrected-read-snm/`, `existing-read-snm/` and `scripts/characterize_bitcell.py` were deleted; GPDK 18nm schematic flow is `scripts/check_snm_sweep.py` + `scripts/butterfly_snm.py` (0.8V nominal).
* Supply behaviour at 0.7 V / 0.5 V is unknown until the §4e sweep runs.
* No corner (SS/FF, temperature) or mismatch analysis yet.
* Area, power, and timing carry no estimates — measurement only.

TIP: What limits the cell, and what changes for Part 2, belongs in
the Part 1 report §6 Discussion and is mirrored here once written.
