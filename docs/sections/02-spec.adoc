== Specification

The unit computes a 16-element dot product of 4-bit unsigned weights stem:[W] and activations stem:[X]:

[stem]
++++
\mathrm{ACC} = \sum_{i=0}^{15} W_{i} \cdot X_{i}
++++

The maximum unsigned sum is stem:[16\times15\times15=3600], which fits in 12 bits. Two copies of the Part 2 16×4 macro hold W and X. A static-CMOS 4×4 array multiplier, a 12-bit ripple-carry accumulator, a 4-bit address counter and a two-bit controller sequence the 16 products. Every register is a transmission-gate master-slave flip-flop. The read is pipelined against the multiply-accumulate (address and read in cycle n, accumulate in n+1), so a dot product takes **17 cycles** from START to DONE (handout limit: 20).

=== Technology and flow

* Cadence GPDK 18 nm, Virtuoso schematic and (Part 1) custom layout.
* Spectre / ADE / ViVA. Part 1 characterization is extracted. Parts 2 and 3 in this report are schematic-level (no extracted parasitics on the MAC).
* Nominal stem:[V_{DD}=0.8\,\mathrm{V}], 27 °C. Part 1 also reports 0.7 V and 0.5 V.
* Density factor stem:[D=28] from the laid-out cell: one fin = 0.048 µm × 18 nm = 0.0242 µm².
