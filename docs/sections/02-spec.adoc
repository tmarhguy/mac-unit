== Specification

System overview: what the unit computes, how the work splits into
three graded parts, and where each piece lives in the tree.

=== Dot-product specification

The unit computes a 16-element dot product of 4-bit unsigned
weights stem:[W] and activations stem:[X]:

[stem]
++++
\mathrm{ACC} = \sum_{i=0}^{15} W[i] \cdot X[i]
++++

Blocks:

* two 16 × 4 SRAM macros (weights, activations)
* one 4×4 unsigned multiplier
* one 12-bit accumulator (max sum: 16 × 15 × 15 = 3600 < 4096)
* one sequencer stepping through the 16 index positions

=== Technology and flow

* Cadence GPDK 18 nm process, Virtuoso schematic and layout.
* Spectre / ADE for characterization and transient checks.
* DRC, LVS, and PEX required; characterization is post-layout.
* Nominal supply stem:[V_{DD} = 1.2\,\mathrm{V}];
supply sweep additionally covers 0.9 V and 0.6 V for Part 1 §4e.
